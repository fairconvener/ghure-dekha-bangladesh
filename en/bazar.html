Common questions

What is each district of Bangladesh famous for?

This page lists the famous food and products of all 64 districts by division: Bogura's doi (yoghurt), Natore's kanchagolla, Rajshahi's mangoes, Tangail's chomchom and more.

How do I add my own product?

Press "➕ Add your product", then give the district, your shop or page name, what you sell and a Facebook page or a mobile number. It is free, needs no login and shows to everyone at once.

Are the sellers checked?

No. Sellers add their own details. Make sure before you buy; if something looks fake or offensive, report it. After several people report an entry, it is removed by itself.
