Where the numbers come from. Each number is copied from an official Bangladesh government website (.gov.bd): the national portal, the police, the Tourist Police, the fire service and the district courts; what 999 covers is from news reports. We keep the official numbers of posts (the officer in charge, the duty officer, a station or office), not officers' own mobiles, and leave out any number that looked like a misprint.

Nobody can add or change a number on this page. If a number is wrong or does not answer, tell us below and we will check it against the source. In danger, call 999 first.

Common questions

What is the emergency number in Bangladesh?

Call 999 in any emergency: the police, the fire service and an ambulance through one number. The call is free; the ambulance itself is not.

What is the Tourist Police number?

The Tourist Police 24-hour helpline is 01320-222222 or 01887-878787. This page also lists the local tourist police offices of Cox's Bazar, Rangamati, Sylhet and Moulvibazar.

What is the fire service number?

The fire service hotline is 102; 999 also reaches the fire service.

Where can I find the number of a police station?

The police stations of Cox's Bazar, Bandarban, Rangamati, Sylhet and Moulvibazar are on this page and on each district's travel guide, with the official source of every number.

Which number is for health services?

The health call centre of the Directorate General of Health Services, 16263, answers 24 hours. For an ambulance in an emergency, call 999.
