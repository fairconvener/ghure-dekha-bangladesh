Sources Where the information comes from

Maps, train times, roads, photos, district guides and emergency numbers: where each comes from, on one page, with a link to every source. If something looks wrong, tell us with the 👎 button under that part of the page.

Map boundaries

  • geoBoundariesCC BY 4.0

    The 64-district map, the business coverage maps, partner tour maps, and with GADM the all-upazila map.

    www.geoboundaries.org
  • Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) and OCHA, 2023, via HDXCC BY 3.0 IGO

    The upazila maps of each district (/zila), the Bangladesh map among the country maps, and district borders in the trip planner.

    data.humdata.org
  • GADM 4.1GADM licence

    The map of all 545 upazilas and metro thanas (/upazila), and a list of upazila names in Bangla.

    gadm.org
  • Natural EarthPublic domain

    The world map, country and continent maps, the Hajj and Umrah map, and some railway lines in the trip planner.

    www.naturalearthdata.com
  • nuhil/bangladesh-geocodeMIT

    Matching the Bangla names of upazilas on the district maps.

    github.com

Train times

  • Bangladesh Railway e-ticket timetable

    Every train timetable page and the trip planner. Fetched on 6 October 2026; times can change.

    eticket.railway.gov.bd
  • Bangla Wikipedia: list of passenger trains of BangladeshCC BY-SA

    The trains-from-Dhaka card on each district guide.

    bn.wikipedia.org

Planner roads and distances

  • OpenStreetMap© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL

    Roads, waterways, railway lines and stations on the trip planner map.

    www.openstreetmap.org
  • OSRM

    Road distance and driving time between district towns, worked out once from OpenStreetMap data.

    project-osrm.org
  • WikidataCC0

    Railway stations on the trip planner map.

    www.wikidata.org

Photos

  • Wikimedia CommonsLicence on each photo

    Photos of districts and places. Each photo shows its photographer and licence; tap it to open the photo's own page. The Hajj and Umrah photos of Makkah and Madinah are CC0.

    commons.wikimedia.org

District guides

  • Written for this site; last updated October 2026. Hotel prices are estimates. Readers correct each part with 👍 and 👎; the famous food line of each district comes from its guide.
