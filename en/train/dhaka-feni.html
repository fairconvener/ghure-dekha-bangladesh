Common questions

How many trains run from Dhaka to Feni?

According to the Bangladesh Railway e-ticket timetable, 4 direct trains run from Dhaka (Kamalapur) to Feni: Mahanagar Provati (704), Chattala Express (802), Mohanagar Express (722) and Turna (742).

When do the first and last trains leave Dhaka for Feni?

The first train of the day from Dhaka (Kamalapur) to Feni, Mahanagar Provati (704), leaves at 7:45 am, and the last, Turna (742), leaves at 11:15 pm.

How long does the train from Dhaka to Feni take?

The train from Dhaka to Feni takes 4 hours 15 minutes to 4 hours 38 minutes; the fastest is Mahanagar Provati (704), at 4 hours 15 minutes.

On which days of the week do the trains from Dhaka to Feni not run?

Weekly off days of the trains from Dhaka to Feni: Mahanagar Provati (704) runs every day; Chattala Express (802) does not run on Friday; Mohanagar Express (722) does not run on Sunday; Turna (742) runs every day.
