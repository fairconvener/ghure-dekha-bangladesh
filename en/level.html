    How the levels work

    LevelWhen to choose itPoints
    Lived thereLived there for a while: home, study or work5
    Stayed the nightSpent at least one night there4
    VisitedGot out and looked around, but did not stay the night3
    StoppedGot off for a short while: a break on the journey, a meal or a quick errand2
    Passed throughOnly passed through by bus, train or launch, without getting off1
    Never beenNot been there yet0

    If you have been to a district many times, give it the highest level that fits. 64 districts × 5 = 320 at most.
