Gopalganj travel guide

Dhaka Division · 5 places to see · 1-day trip
Best timeOctober to March
Days needed1 day
District townGopalganj
From Dhaka4-5 hours by bus

At a glance: Gopalganj is a district in Dhaka Division; the best time to visit is October to March; a visit takes 1 day; from Dhaka it takes 4-5 hours by bus.

Last updated: October 2026

What to see in Gopalganj, how to get there, where to stay and what to eat, on one page. Been there and found something wrong? Tap 👎 under that part and tell us what is right; if it is right, tap 👍.

1

What to see

1

Bangabandhu Mausoleum

🏛️ Heritage

The tomb complex of the Father of the Nation, with a museum and library; beside the Baigar River.

2

Orakandi Thakurbari

🕌 Religious site

The holy seat of the Matua community; hundreds of thousands come to the Baruni bathing fair in Chaitra (March-April).

3

Ujani Zamindar Bari

🏛️ Heritage

Ruins of an old zamindar house and a temple in Muksudpur.

4

Madhumati River

🚣 River

The river flowing past the town and a British-era canal; afternoons on a boat.

5

Sukanta Bhattacharya's Ancestral Home

🏛️ Heritage

The ancestral home of Sukanta Bhattacharya, poet of 'Chharpatra', in Unshia village, Kotalipara.

4

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Gopalganj?

The best time to visit Gopalganj is October to March.

How do I get from Dhaka to Gopalganj?

Ways to get from Dhaka to Gopalganj: 4-5 hours by bus (from Gabtoli), or 3 hours via the Padma Bridge. Trains from Dhaka that stop in Gopalganj district: Jahanabad Express and Ruposhi Bangla Express.

What is there to see in Gopalganj?

Places to see in Gopalganj: Bangabandhu Mausoleum, Orakandi Thakurbari, Ujani Zamindar Bari, Madhumati River and Sukanta Bhattacharya's Ancestral Home.

How many days does it take to see Gopalganj?

It takes 1 day to see Gopalganj.

Where can I stay in Gopalganj?

Accommodation in Gopalganj: mid-range hotels in town, Tk 800-2,000 (approx.).

What is the famous food of Gopalganj?

The famous food of Gopalganj: Gopalganj's rasmalai (cheese balls in cream), chomchom (milk sweet) and other sweets.

Who do I call in an emergency in Gopalganj?

Call 999 for the police, the fire service or an ambulance anywhere in Bangladesh; the call is free. The Tourist Police 24-hour helpline is 01320-222222, and the fire service hotline is 102.
